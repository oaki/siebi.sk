# Expansion Plan — Siebi s.r.o.

Internal document. Not published on the site. Builds directly on [market-analysis.md](./market-analysis.md) — read that first for the reasoning behind each phase.

## Phase 0 — Get found (now, this build)

Ship the sk/en/de site focused purely on sieťotlač + ADR nálepky. Success here isn't traffic volume, it's **being findable by the right narrow query** — "ADR nálepky sieťotlač," "výroba ADR označenia kovové sudy," German/English equivalents. Depth on the ADR page (why screen-printed beats adhesive, material list, durability claims) is what earns both search ranking and AI-answer-engine citations.

*Exit condition*: site live, indexed, ranking for the core ADR/sieťotlač terms in Slovak within a few months.

## Phase 1 — Make the durability differentiation impossible to miss

Market analysis found the real edge is: screen-printed ink is chemically bonded to the surface, adhesive stickers delaminate under abrasion/chemical exposure. Most competitors (the sticker e-shops) can't make this claim. Right now that's a paragraph of text — it should become proof:

- Real photos of labels/panels **after months or years in service** (weathered, still legible) — the single most persuasive asset this business could produce, and something a sticker e-shop structurally cannot show.
- A short, honest "prečo nie samolepka" (why not a sticker) comparison — not disparaging competitors by name, just explaining the physical difference.
- Ask current clients (Chirana Dental, EKOM, DoNaTio) if any would give a one-line testimonial or allow a case-study photo — real client validation is rare in this space and highly credible.

*Exit condition*: at least 2–3 real "in service" photos and one client testimonial on the site.

## Phase 2 — DACH market push

ADR is EU-wide and the hazard symbols are standardized, so German/Austrian demand is real, not aspirational (this is *why* the /de/ site exists). Once Phase 1's proof points exist to translate:

- Confirm the /de/ content is genuinely complete (not thin), matching the sk depth — already a requirement from the build, but worth re-auditing once Phase 1 content lands.
- Identify a handful of German/Austrian logistics or chemical-transport trade directories/associations to be listed in (low-cost, high-relevance backlinks and direct discovery).
- Consider whether German-language customer support (phone/email) capacity exists before pushing this hard — a DE-language site attracting DE-language inquiries that can't be answered in German risks a bad first impression.

*Exit condition*: first inbound inquiry from a DE/AT prospect, handled successfully.

## Phase 3 — Validate the battery/waste-recycling segment

The 2026–2027 regulatory tightening on battery/dangerous-waste transport labeling (vyhláška 89/2024, lithium battery reclassification) is a real tailwind, but Siebi's current client list doesn't obviously include this segment yet — it's a hypothesis, not a confirmed fit.

- A handful of direct outreach calls/emails to battery-recycling or waste-management operators in Slovakia, framed as "does your current labeling survive X" — cheap to test, would validate or kill this segment before spending marketing effort on it.
- Only if validated: a dedicated content section addressing this segment's specific compliance language.

*Exit condition*: either a confirmed pipeline lead from this segment, or a documented "not a fit, don't pursue" conclusion — both are useful outcomes.

## Phase 4 — Capacity planning (conditional)

Not a near-term action — a flag for later. If Phases 1–3 generate real demand growth, a small operation (as this appears to be) will hit a capacity ceiling before a marketing ceiling. Revisit production capacity, lead times, and whether the site should start setting expectations on turnaround time once order volume is actually a constraint — premature to plan in detail now.

## What NOT to do

- Don't re-broaden the service catalog (vizitky, tričká, DTP, etc.) chasing revenue diversity — that was deliberately cut for focus, and diluting it undoes the SEO/GEO topical-authority strategy the whole site is built around.
- Don't compete with the adhesive-sticker e-shops on price or speed — that's a different business model with structurally lower costs; competing there means losing on ground Siebi doesn't actually hold.
