# siebi.sk

Marketing/informational website for Siebi s.r.o., a Slovak screen-printing and industrial-marking company. Exists to make the company findable by people who need ADR transport stickers or screen printing (sieťotlač) done in durable, resistant inks — and to present the company across three markets (sk, en, de).

## Language

**Siebi s.r.o.**:
The current legal entity operating the site: IČO 36270211, DIČ 2021993446, seat at Štefánikova 15/1168, 921 01 Piešťany. Owned by Pavol Binčík. Commercially positioned as continuing his father Milan Binčík's screen-printing craft, but legally it is a renamed pre-existing shell company (formerly "FANRAD, spol. s r.o.", founded 2005-04-01, name changed 2026), not a legal successor to Milan Binčík's sole proprietorship. Registered SK NACE codes (insurance-ancillary activities, wholesale intermediation) are leftover from the shell company and do not reflect the actual business — irrelevant to site content.
_Avoid_: Milan Binčík - SIEBI (the predecessor business — real craft/heritage reference, but not this legal entity)

**ADR nálepky**:
Warning/hazard stickers required for labeling vehicles and containers transporting dangerous goods under the ADR (European Agreement concerning the International Carriage of Dangerous Goods by Road) convention. The primary product focus of the site.
_Avoid_: hazard labels, warning stickers (used generically; "ADR nálepky/stickers" is the precise term)

**Sieťotlač**:
Screen printing — the company's *only* service (not one line among several). Applied to foils, plastics, metal, glass, and self-adhesive materials with durable, chemical- and weather-resistant (two-component) inks. ADR nálepky is the flagship application of this technique. The old business also offered vizitky (business cards), potlač tričiek (t-shirt printing), DTP/grafický návrh, svetelná reklama, etc. — Siebi s.r.o. deliberately does **not** carry these forward.
_Avoid_: potlač (too generic — potlač is any kind of printing, sieťotlač is the specific screen-printing technique)

**Dvojzložkové farby**:
Two-component (chemically-cured) paints/inks. The actual technical mechanism behind the "odolné farby" (durable/resistant paint) claim — used on non-standard materials (metal, glass) to protect the surface and extend product lifespan; historically pitched as superior to tampoprint (pad printing).
_Avoid_: kvalitné farby (vague marketing language — "dvojzložkové" is the precise, credible technical term)

**Veľkoformátová (sieťotlač)**:
Large-format *screen-printed* jobs (big panels, tabule, foil sheets) — a size/format descriptor within sieťotlač, not a separate technique. The old business's separate "Veľkoformátové fotografie" line (digital/inkjet large-format photo printing) is discontinued and not part of Siebi s.r.o.'s offering.
_Avoid_: veľkoformátová tlač used alone/ambiguously — always tie it to sieťotlač so it isn't read as the old digital photo-printing service
